SOURCE MATCHING MANIFEST
record_type: development for text-model development; main for the analysis sample.
record_id: source_pilot_row for development; analysis_id for main.
category: original Amazon review category.
parent_asin: source product identifier used for exact text lookup.
text_sha256: SHA-256 of the UTF-8 review body.

DEVELOPMENT SPLIT MANIFEST
source_pilot_row: retained development-record identifier.
parent_asin: source product identifier used to enforce disjoint product splits.
rating_num: posted rating used as the supervised target, integer 1 to 5.
category: original Amazon review category.
split: train, validation or test.
validation_role: calibration or selection for validation records; empty otherwise.

The matching files contain public product identifiers and text hashes. They
allow records to be linked to the source collection. They are not described
as irreversibly anonymised. They contain no direct user identifiers or text.
